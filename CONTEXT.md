# Personal Spending

This context describes how one person records and understands personal spending against self-defined monthly limits.

## Language

**Pocket**:
A spending classification that also carries a user-defined monthly budget; expenses assigned to it reduce the amount available for that month.
_Avoid_: Category, envelope, account

**Pocket availability**:
The portion of a pocket's monthly budget that has not yet been consumed by its recorded expenses.
_Avoid_: Account balance, bank balance
